Beach Creative

1:03

A fully self-produced commercial creative, developed and executed from capture to final delivery.

I was responsible for the production process, including creative direction, performance direction, cinematography, camera operation, lighting, shot composition and visual storytelling.

Working with a minimal production setup on location at the beach, I directed the talent's performance, movement and presence on camera, while designing the shots and visual approach around the environment and available natural light.

I also handled the complete post-production, including editing, pacing, sound design, color grading, transitions, visual effects and final image treatment.
