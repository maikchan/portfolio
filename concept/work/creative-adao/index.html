Creative Adão

0:43

A project developed and executed entirely by me, from the initial idea to the final cut.

I was responsible for the concept, direction, performance direction, cinematography, lighting and camera operation, shaping both the visual approach and the performance on set.

The production was designed around deliberate framing, camera movement and lighting, using the location and the subject to build a stronger visual identity rather than simply documenting the performance.

I also handled the complete post-production, including editing, pacing, sound design, motion graphics and color grading. Each stage was approached as part of the same creative process, with decisions made during production already considering how the material would ultimately work in the edit.
