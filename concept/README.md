# Visual concept

This is a parallel, local review version of the portfolio at `/concept/`. The existing site at `/` is unchanged. The concept includes Home, Work, Services, About, and all six project pages. Its pages have `noindex,follow` metadata and are absent from the sitemap.

The study keeps the existing project data and media assets. It has its own snapshots of the base stylesheet and site scripts, plus `style.css` for the new direction. The build script copies current page content into the parallel routes, applies the new portraits and routes, and refreshes those snapshots. Running it again overwrites generated files inside `/concept/`, so preserve any later edits to those files first.

Visual decisions: `mm-21.jpg` leads the desktop hero and `mm-33.jpg` leads mobile. The desktop portrait sits farther right, with a dark text field and a stronger vignette around the room edges. The subject is brighter without lifting the edges. On fine pointers, light follows the right side of the portrait, stays while the cursor rests there, and fades when it leaves. Hero copy fades as the section leaves the viewport; after a full exit, returning reveals the kicker, headline lines, copy, actions, and capabilities in a staggered left-to-right wipe. On mobile, the portrait also moves slightly with scroll. Reduced-motion settings disable these effects. The original black-and-white portrait is the primary image on the full About page. The photo under the tree leads the Home About gallery, followed by the workstation photo and `mm-32.jpg`. The original red-shirt portrait anchors the left of the One Project section, opposite a new typographic hierarchy on the right. That section also has restrained pointer parallax on desktop. Brass and dark blue carry the interface; decorative red and offset color blocks have been removed. Oswald, Space Grotesk, and Geist Mono remain the type system with stronger scale and spacing.

The showreel requests muted autoplay. Small outlined controls below the frame show the current playback and sound states, with their actions available through accessible labels. The poster remains visible while the embedded player loads, and an external fallback link appears if it does not load. Playback depends on the visitor's browser and Vimeo availability.

Scroll entrances use a small shared vocabulary: section headings reveal horizontally, film and portrait frames open vertically, and service blocks move in slightly from the left. Project cards and work-mode blocks keep their quieter rise. Each entrance plays once, the delays are short, and reduced-motion visitors see the content immediately.

Review locally at `/concept/`; compare with `/`. The footer of every concept page links back to the current site. The concept has not been published or added to production navigation.
